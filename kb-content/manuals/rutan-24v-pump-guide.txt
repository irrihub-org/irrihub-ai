RUTAN 24V 250W SOLAR PUMP — QUICK REFERENCE GUIDE

MODEL: Rutan 24V DC Submersible Solar Pump
POWER: 250W
VOLTAGE: 24V DC
MAX FLOW: 3,000 litres/hour
MAX HEAD: 40 metres
SUITABLE FOR: Farms up to 5 acres with drip or sprinkler irrigation

INSTALLATION
1. Mount solar panels on a south-facing frame at 15-degree tilt angle
2. Connect panels in series to achieve 24V output (2x 12V panels or 1x 24V panel)
3. Connect panel output to the pump controller input terminals
4. Connect controller output to pump motor terminals
5. Lower pump into water source — minimum 30cm water above pump inlet
6. Prime the system by running for 5 minutes with outlet valve open
7. Connect main irrigation line to pump outlet

TROUBLESHOOTING
Problem: Pump does not start
- Check solar panel voltage with multimeter — should read 24V+ in direct sunlight
- Check all cable connections are tight and not corroded
- Check controller LED — green = normal, red = fault, no light = no power
- Check fuse on controller board

Problem: Pump runs but low flow
- Check for blockages in the suction strainer
- Check water level — pump must be submerged
- Check for air leaks in delivery pipe
- Clean or replace the impeller if worn

Problem: Pump starts and stops repeatedly
- Insufficient sunlight — panels need direct sun, not partial shade
- Water level too low — pump cycling on dry-run protection
- Controller overheating — ensure ventilation around controller box

MAINTENANCE SCHEDULE
Weekly: Check cable connections, clean panel surface
Monthly: Inspect suction strainer, check system pressure
Quarterly: Flush all irrigation lines, inspect emitter performance
Annually: Full system inspection, replace worn seals, check impeller
